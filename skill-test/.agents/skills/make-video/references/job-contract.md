# Experimental job contract

All paths are relative to `skill-test/`.

```text
jobs/<slug>/brief.json
jobs/<slug>/research.md
jobs/<slug>/claims.json
jobs/<slug>/assets.json
jobs/<slug>/video-plan.json
jobs/<slug>/timing.json
jobs/<slug>/runtime.json
jobs/<slug>/qa.json
jobs/<slug>/output/review.mp4
jobs/<slug>/output/video.mp4
starter-hf/<slug>/index.html
starter-hf/<slug>/assets/
starter-hf/<slug>/voice/
starter-hf/<slug>/qa/
```

`brief.json` has `topic`, `audience`, `viewer_promise`, and `visual_direction`.
`claims.json` is `{ "schemaVersion": 1, "claims": [...] }`. A supported
claim has `id`, `text`, `status`, `source_url`, `source_title`, exact
`excerpt`, and `confidence`; an illustrative claim has `id`, `text`, and
`status`.

`assets.json` is `{ "schemaVersion": 1, "assets": [...] }`. Every asset has
`id`, `source_url`, `runtime_path` relative to `starter-hf/<slug>/`,
`evidence_role` (`primary`, `contextual`, or `illustrative`), `claim_ids`,
`license_status`, `rights_text`, and `attribution_note`.

`video-plan.json` is `{ "schemaVersion": 2, "video": {}, "visual_language":
{}, "chapters": [], "beats": [] }`. `video` sets `aspect_ratio: "9:16"` and a
positive `target_duration_seconds`. `visual_language` turns the user visual
brief into a `concept`, `media_strategy`, `typography_role`, and explicit
`negative_patterns`. It contains only claim/asset ids, not claim text, URLs,
or asset license data.

Every beat has the normal story fields plus a unique `visual_shot`: `id`,
`focal_subject`, `visual_family`, `max_non_caption_text`, and
`continuity_intent`. A beat maps to one primary visible shot; it may not be
hidden inside a longer shared HUD/panel scene.

`timing.json` is generated by TTS. It is the only timing source for captions,
snapshot timestamps, and composition start/duration values.
